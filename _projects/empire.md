[**EMPIRE**](https://www.sandia.gov/news/publications/hpc-annual-reports/article/empire/) (ElectroMagnetic Plasma In Realistic Environments) is Sandia National Laboratories' plasma modelling capability for pulsed power research -- most visibly for predicting how energy is delivered to, and lost within, accelerator architectures such as the [Z Machine](https://www.sandia.gov/z-machine/).

## My role

I worked on EMPIRE-PIC from 2015 as a Research Fellow at Warwick, within the Centre for Computational Plasma Physics and in collaboration with Sandia and UK AWE. In 2016 I was seconded to Sandia as a **Visiting Research Fellow**, leading the Warwick effort on the Kokkos-accelerated particle-in-cell elements of the application {% cite bettencourt:2021:empire-pic %}.

At Warwick, we led the development of high-order particle representations within the PIC simulation, trading arithmetic per particle for a substantial reduction in particle count and noise {% cite brown:2021:higher-order-particle-representation brown:2017:ho-cppc %}.
