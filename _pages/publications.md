## Books

**An Introduction to Parallel Programming (Third Edition)**
Peter S. Pacheco, Matthew Malensek and Steven A. Wright.
Morgan Kaufmann, 2026. _In production._

The third edition of Pacheco's widely used undergraduate text on parallel programming, on which I join as an author. The book teaches parallel programming from first principles across Pthreads, OpenMP, MPI, and CUDA. The [second edition](https://www.sciencedirect.com/book/monograph/9780128046050/an-introduction-to-parallel-programming) is available from Elsevier.
