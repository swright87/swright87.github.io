Service

PMBS

I co-chair the International Workshop on Performance Modeling, Benchmarking and Simulation of High Performance Computer Systems (PMBS), held annually in conjunction with the ACM/IEEE Supercomputing conference. I have been on the organising committee since 2013 and have co-edited every proceedings since.

PMBS brings together researchers from academia, industry and the national laboratories to discuss the performance modelling, analysis, and prediction of applications and of the systems they run on – including the concerns that have grown to dominate the field, such as power consumption and reliability.

The proceedings have appeared as Springer Lecture Notes in Computer Science volumes (8551, 8966, 10724), as ACM SIGHPC proceedings, and latterly within the SC Workshops proceedings published by ACM and IEEE. Associated special issues have been published in Parallel Computing (2025), Concurrency and Computation: Practice and Experience (vol. 34, 2022) and Future Generation Computer Systems (vol. 92, 2018).
