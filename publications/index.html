Publications

Books

An Introduction to Parallel Programming (Third Edition) Peter S. Pacheco, Matthew Malensek and Steven A. Wright. Morgan Kaufmann, 2026. In production.

The third edition of Pacheco’s widely used undergraduate text on parallel programming, on which I join as an author. The book teaches parallel programming from first principles across Pthreads, OpenMP, MPI, and CUDA. The second edition is available from Elsevier.
